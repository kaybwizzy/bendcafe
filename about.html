About The Bend 25

A quiet standard, kept every day.

Our Story

Named for the bend in the road that leads to it.

The Bend 25 Cafe sits at a small turn in the city — easy to miss if you're not looking, unforgettable once you've found it. We built this room around a simple belief: that good food, properly made coffee, and genuine hospitality don't need to shout to be felt.

Every dish on our menu is adapted from classics found around the world, then rebuilt around what's freshest at our local markets that week. Nothing is rushed — not the food, not the coffee, and not you.

"Hospitality, to us, is noticing what a guest needs before they have to ask for it."
— The Bend 25 Cafe
