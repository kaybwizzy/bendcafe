Our Story

A corner of Kampala built for lingering.

What began as a single bend in the road became a gathering place — for early meetings over flat whites, long lunches with old friends, and quiet afternoons with a good book. Every dish is adapted from classics found around the world, plated with care, and served in a room designed to feel like your best-kept secret.
